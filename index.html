About 01 / 06

Reading the city at street level

Planning training, public-sector practice, cross-border research — and an advocacy junction in between.

My work moves between three roles: researcher, planner, and advocate. I was trained in planning at National Taiwan University, managed urban regeneration and social housing projects for the Taipei City Government, and then moved to the Netherlands for a joint master's in Metropolitan Analysis, Design and Engineering (MADE) at TU Delft, Wageningen University and the AMS Institute in Amsterdam.

The questions stay the same wherever I am: how does a street get decided, whose mobility gets prioritised, and can citizens take part in those decisions in their own language? Lately I use open data, GIS and generative AI to translate pedestrian environments, road safety and healthy-city planning methods into interfaces that ordinary people can read and use.

Since 2024 I have co-founded Vision Zero Taiwan, where I serve as Executive Supervisor, working on mobility transition policy research and international collaboration.
